Quartermaster CSV guide

Start from quartermaster-template.csv or Download CSV. Replace the sample data
with known facts; do not invent missing information. Share the file and website
link: Crew > Import CSV loads an independent copy. Changes do not sync.

File format

Use UTF-8. Keep the version row, table headings, and column order. Quote cells
containing commas or quotes, and double any quotes inside them. Blank rows are OK.
Start with:
Quartermaster CSV,1
Quartermaster trip,Your trip name
Currency,USD

IDs link the tables. Use unique crew IDs (p1, p2, etc.) and cost IDs (e1, e2, etc.).
Update their references if changed. Amounts are decimals with at most two decimal
places: no currency symbols, thousands separators, percentages, or formulas.

Source tables

Keep these exact headings and columns:

CREW
ID,Name
- One row per person, including anyone with no costs. Names must be distinct
  and 1–40 characters long.

SETTLEMENT GROUPS
Group,Member IDs
- Optional. In Member IDs, list at least two crew IDs separated by | (p1|p2).
  Each person can join one group. Group names are derived from member names.

EXPENSES
Expense,Date,Category,Paid by,Amount,Split,ID,Split with (crew IDs)
- Expense: a description of 1–100 characters.
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD or blank.
- Category: stays, meals, transport, activities, groceries, or other.
- Paid by: an exact CREW name; update it when renaming someone. The payer need
  not share the cost.
- Amount: positive. Split: even, individual (one person), or itemized (receipt).
- Split with: crew IDs separated by |. Their order breaks rounding ties.

RECEIPT DETAILS — already included in expense totals above
Receipt,Item / extra,Amount,Expense ID,Type,Split with (crew IDs)
- Required for itemized costs. Expense ID links to the cost's ID in EXPENSES.
- Type item: a name (1–100 characters), positive amount, and one or more crew IDs.
- Type tax and tip: exactly one row of each per receipt. Use monetary amounts,
  0.00 for none, and leave Split with empty.
- Import recalculates receipt totals and participants from these details.

Member share columns, TOTAL, and balance/payment tables are calculated output;
you can omit them when preparing a file. Import and download again to refresh them.

Calculations

- Total cost: sum EXPENSES rows, excluding TOTAL. Receipt details are already
  included; do not count them twice.
- For each person, sum their allocated cost shares. Balance = paid minus share.
  Positive receives; negative owes.
- Ordinary costs and receipt items split evenly among their listed people.
  Extra pennies follow participant order.
- Tax/tip share = extra × person's allocated item subtotal ÷ receipt item subtotal.
  Round down to cents, then give remaining pennies to the largest fractional
  remainders. Ties follow first appearance in item participant lists.
- Groups combine individual balances for settlement. Suggested payments describe
  how to settle; the app does not send or track payments.

Limits and safety

- Trip name: up to 80 characters. One currency: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, NZD,
  CHF, INR, or SGD. Convert foreign costs first.
- Maximum: 50 people, 100 items per receipt, 1,000 costs/items combined,
  999999999.99 per cost/receipt including extras, and 8,000,000 bytes per file.
- Names and descriptions cannot contain line breaks or control characters.
- Prefix text with an apostrophe if it starts with an apostrophe, or with
  =, +, -, @ (including full-width forms) after optional whitespace. Keep existing
  export prefixes; import decodes them.
- Spreadsheet apps may strip escaping when saving. Keep the original export
  and load untrusted cells as text when opening them in a spreadsheet.
- Quartermaster never executes imported formulas or code. Imports are checked
  locally and previewed; review the people and amounts before replacing a trip.
